CheckDrive — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

CheckDrive (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a car-trip checklist app. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using CheckDrive you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

Your checklists, trip history, and app settings are stored locally on your device only. We do not operate accounts and we do not upload, sync, or store this content on our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Bluetooth / vehicle detection

If you enable automatic car detection, the app uses Bluetooth to recognize when your phone connects to or disconnects from a car you choose. This detection happens entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your Bluetooth devices, pairing information, or location.

3. Information collected by third parties

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising). To show ads, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, approximate (non-precise) location, device identifiers, and ad-interaction data. See Google’s Privacy Policy. Ads are shown only to users who have not purchased the ad-free upgrade.
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat (purchases). When you buy the one-time Pro upgrade, your purchase is processed by Google Play, and purchase status is managed through RevenueCat using an anonymous app-generated identifier. We never see or store your payment-card details. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

With your permission, the app sends local notifications (for example, a back-seat reminder when you leave your car). These are generated on your device. You can turn them off in your device settings at any time.

5. How information is used

Data handled by the app is used only to provide its features (checklists, reminders, vehicle detection), to display advertising, and to process and restore purchases. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device’s settings.
  • In the European Economic Area and the UK, the app presents a consent prompt for personalized advertising before ads load.
  • You can purchase the Pro upgrade to remove ads entirely.
  • You can revoke Bluetooth or notification permissions in device settings.

7. Children

CheckDrive is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
