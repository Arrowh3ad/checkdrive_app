Frequently asked

How does car detection work?

CheckDrive can remind you to run a checklist when you get in the car. On Android, it watches for your car’s Bluetooth connection. On iPhone, you set up a quick one-time Shortcuts automation. Open Settings → Car detection setup in the app for step-by-step instructions for your device.

I bought Pro on a new phone — how do I get it back?

Open the CheckDrive Pro screen and tap Restore purchase. Your one-time Pro unlock is tied to your app store account and can be restored at no extra cost.

Where is my data stored?

Your checklists and trip history are stored only on your device — there is no account and nothing is uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app removes this data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting a checklist or clearing trip history in the app removes that data immediately. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device.
